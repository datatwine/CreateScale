Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

ArtKhoj ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Stage Freedom. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and profession you provide when creating your profile.
  • Profile content — photos, bio, location, and portfolio media you upload.
  • Booking data — engagement details (date, venue, occasion) created between performers and clients.
  • Device identifiers — advertising ID and basic device info collected by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, features used, and error logs collected to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and improve the ArtKhoj platform.
  • Display your profile to other users so they can discover and book you.
  • Process bookings and facilitate communication between performers and clients.
  • Serve ads through Google AdMob (see Section 4).
  • Send service-related notifications (booking confirmations, messages).

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers hosted in AWS (ap-south-1, Mumbai). Uploaded media files are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — collects device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — provides CDN, DNS, and R2 object storage for media files.
  • Razorpay — processes payments. Payment details are handled directly by Razorpay and are not stored on our servers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Booking records are kept for 3 years after the engagement date for legal and accounting purposes. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

ArtKhoj is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by email. Continued use of ArtKhoj after changes constitutes acceptance.
